# Title Design Reference

Use this reference after the article draft and paragraph revision are mostly complete. Title design must be based on the finished article's real claim, reader pain, personal scene, and evidence. Do not invent a stronger conflict than the article supports.

## Benchmark titles

When the user supplies a benchmark title set they have permission to analyze, extract only reusable title structures. Do not ship another creator's title corpus, quote a source title as a new title, or carry performance claims without data.

## Extracted Title Grammar

Use these grammars as generative patterns. Match them to the article's real content.

1. Authority or named concept + sharp judgment
   - Pattern: `可信来源或概念 + 与正文证据一致的判断`
   - Use when the article has a credible external thinker, institution, tool, trend, or book-backed idea.

2. The more X, the more Y
   - Pattern: `你越 X，越 Y`
   - Use when the article exposes a counterintuitive trap, such as learning more but becoming more confused.

3. Year + percentage/group + strong forecast
   - Pattern: `2026 年，90% 的 X，为什么会 Y`
   - Use when the article is about trend judgment, market change, or a group-level warning.

4. Why-question with counterintuitive result
   - Pattern: `为什么 X，反而 Y？？？`
   - Use when the article answers a real pain point or common confusion.

5. Identity label + hidden fate
   - Pattern: `普通人 / 老板 / 文科生 / 自媒体人 / 职场新人 + 最大问题`
   - Use when the title should make the reader feel named.

6. Strong dissuasion or threshold
   - Pattern: `连 X 都不敢，那你还是别 Y 了...`
   - Use sparingly when the article's tone is intentionally sharp.

7. Personal transformation and result
   - Pattern: `从 A 到 B，我才开始 C`
   - Use when the article includes a verified personal scene or journey.

8. Method promise with bounded steps
   - Pattern: `X 最完整的系统打法｜保姆级 N 步法`
   - Use for method-list or product-conversion articles. Avoid this when the article is mainly opinion.

9. Sentence-like golden judgment
   - Pattern: `X 越弱，你越像 Y；X 越强，你才会 Z`
   - Use when the article has a memorable conceptual contrast.

10. Money/result + deeper value
    - Pattern: `当你年入 X：真正的意义，是 Y`
    - Use when the article connects practical results to a deeper personal-growth judgment.

## Required Title Design Process

After the draft passes source, personal-scene, endorsement, paragraph, and AI-flavor review:

1. Extract from the article:
   - core judgment
   - reader identity
   - strongest pain
   - counterintuitive point
   - personal scene or user-owned observation
   - endorsement evidence or authority source
   - product/conversion intensity

2. Choose at least 3 title angles:
   - pain resonance
   - counterintuitive trap
   - reader identity
   - authority/thought source
   - personal transformation
   - golden judgment
   - method promise

3. Generate 8-10 title options. Each title should include a short `解析` explaining:
   - title grammar used
   - reader psychological trigger
   - why it fits this article

4. Recommend:
   - 首选标题: strongest fit for this article and platform
   - 备选标题: 2-3 safer alternatives
   - 不建议方向: title angles that would exaggerate, attract the wrong reader, or break article truth

5. Run the title-to-body loop from `editorial-claim-audit.md` for the recommended title. Its emotional conflict or promised judgment must be present in the opening, resolved through the mechanism/turning point, and answered by the ending. Revise the title or body if the loop does not close; do not merely repeat the title phrase three times.

## Constraints

- Do not use emoji.
- Do not copy benchmark titles directly.
- Do not use a named authority unless the article actually uses that source or idea.
- Do not use numbers, income, years, or percentages unless the article supports them.
- Do not overuse `???`, `...`, or hard-sell phrasing; use them only when they match the article's emotional temperature.
- Do not use empty terms such as `赋能`, `闭环`, `抓手`, or `底层逻辑` unless the article translates them into lived language.
- Do not let the title promise a method if the article is mainly a judgment essay.
- Do not make the title more aggressive than the article.
